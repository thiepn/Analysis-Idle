# Prestige

## Result

Global prestige is deferred until a multi-chapter prototype proves a unique purpose.

## Method

Deterministic model `phase0-design-lab/1.0.0`; no randomness or timestamp enters the output. Inputs, candidate metrics, criteria weights, rankings, and sensitivity results are preserved in [`prestige-purpose-results.json`](./data/prestige-purpose-results.json). Scores are explained heuristics, while numerical allocation and offline outputs are enumerated calculations.

## Limitation

No reset formula or timing can be validated from one chapter. This evidence narrows implementation choices; it does not measure fun.
