# Pacing

## Result

The first Publication targets 120–180 active-equivalent minutes over 1–3 sessions.

## Method

Deterministic model `phase0-design-lab/1.0.0`; no randomness or timestamp enters the output. Inputs, candidate metrics, criteria weights, rankings, and sensitivity results are preserved in [`pacing-results.json`](./data/pacing-results.json). Scores are explained heuristics, while numerical allocation and offline outputs are enumerated calculations.

## Limitation

All timing bands are provisional until simulator and human playtests. This evidence narrows implementation choices; it does not measure fun.
