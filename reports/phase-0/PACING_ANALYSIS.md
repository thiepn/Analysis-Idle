# Pacing

## Result

Configure the first Publication for a provisional 60–120 active-equivalent minute band over 1–3 sessions.

## Method

Deterministic model `phase0-design-lab/1.1.0-reconciled`; no randomness or timestamp enters the output. Inputs, candidate metrics, criteria weights, rankings, and sensitivity results are preserved in [`pacing-results.json`](./data/pacing-results.json). Scores are explained heuristics, while numerical allocation and offline outputs are enumerated calculations.

## Limitation

Band scores are design-density heuristics; simulator timelines and human fatigue/comprehension playtests are required. This evidence narrows implementation choices; it does not measure fun.
