## Method

Deterministic model `phase0-design-lab/1.0.0`; no randomness or timestamp enters the output. Inputs, candidate metrics, criteria weights, rankings, and sensitivity results are preserved in [`core-loop-results.json`](./data/core-loop-results.json). ## Limitation

Scores and first-30-minute actions are design estimates, not playtest observations. This evidence narrows implementation choices; it does not measure fun.
