# Content rules

- Stable IDs are immutable; all definitions pass schema, dependency, reachability, and cycle validation.
- Every upgrade states strategic purpose, sources/sinks, effect/stacking/reset behavior, accessibility text, and balance tests.
- Every chapter introduces a behavior-changing mechanic; multiplier-only reskins are rejected.
- Mathematical claims include a content-source note and are reviewed for convention and dependency accuracy.
- Locked, unowned, or unreachable content produces no effects.
