# Open questions

## Resolved Phase 0 evidence gap

The original Deep Research Foundation and Completion Audit are present, checksum-verified in `SOURCE_MANIFEST.json`, and reconciled across 53 decisions. Their presence is no longer a blocker. The remaining questions below are balance, usability, and later-product questions; none requires inventing missing source material.

## Human playtest questions

1. Do new players understand the guided 10-second confirmation and passive production by 60 seconds without mistaking either for a strategic choice?
2. Is the first genuine 3/0 versus 2/1 Attention choice legible within 1–3 minutes, and is the later fourth slot understandable?
3. Does activity exponent 0.80 preserve meaningful specialization without turning “split evenly” into a commandment?
4. Are Precision and Intuition distinct in player language after 20 minutes, or does one feel like a gate currency?
5. Is Technique visibly learned through exercises, prepared steps, and Constructive templates without becoming a third stock or an invisible modifier?
6. Are Formal, Exploratory, and Constructive approaches chosen for downstream behavior rather than ETA?
7. Does stored Insight feel useful at 10–15% without fear of missing out?
8. Does a 60–120-minute active-equivalent first Publication feel substantial but reachable across 1–3 sessions?
9. Does Publication feel like mathematical transformation rather than a reset?
10. Are the three non-power achievement unlocks informative without feeling mandatory?

## Simulator questions

11. Which allocations dominate balanced, bottleneck-first, all-in, no-reallocation, cheapest-project, and approach-specialized policies?
12. Does the selected dedicated project slot outperform the contrary model where projects compete for Attention, without removing meaningful opportunity cost?
13. Would an explicit marginal-rate table produce more viable allocation policies than exponent 0.80?
14. What resource caps keep two visible projects fundable without allowing multi-day prefarming?
15. Which project costs recover from a plausible poor plan within five minutes in the opening hour?
16. How do 4h/24h, 8h/48h, 12h/72h, and 24h/168h offline schedules compare for credited progress and decision overshoot?
17. At what reachable magnitude does the native-number adapter lose economically meaningful increments?

## Deferred product questions

18. Does a multi-chapter prototype reveal a unique global-prestige purpose?
19. Do reusable Formal, Exploratory, and Constructive identities work across three distinct chapters, or should campaign-level branches remain absent?
20. Do measured save size, write cost, and recovery needs ever justify IndexedDB as the sole canonical save store?
21. When do update/save tests justify a PWA, and when does audio add accessible feedback without becoming required?
