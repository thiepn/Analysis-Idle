## Strongly supported by both research inputs

- The v2 direction should be a clean, deterministic, local-first rewrite that preserves v1 rather than incrementally refactoring its frame-coupled economy.
- Scarce allocation, dedicated projects, and mathematical dependencies form a viable core-loop hypothesis when introduced through progressive disclosure.
- Automation must be universal quality-of-life infrastructure, not the exclusive reward of one branch or player type.
- Active play should offer bounded, decision-relevant advantage without repeatable production clicking or timing pressure.
- Offline progress must execute an explicit saved plan, stop safely at unresolved decisions, and explain what happened on return.
- Publication should create semantic compression and reusable methods; it should not be a generic multiplier reset.
- Engine/content must remain independent of UI, wall clock, persistence, and presentation.
- Mathematical truth, accessible presentation, recoverability, and stable save/content IDs are product constraints rather than polish.

## Supported by Phase 0 comparative evidence

- Candidate F, a staged hybrid of whole Attention, a dedicated project slot, and simplified dependencies, ranks best across the recorded heuristic weight sets. This is a selection for implementation testing, not proof of fun.
- Three starting Attention with a later fourth slot minimizes opening controls while still enabling additional policies. The exact 3→4 values remain provisional.
- Two primary stocks plus visible method preparation produces clearer source/sink separation than one, three, or four primary stocks in the current matrix.
- Reversible Formal, Exploratory, and Constructive approaches preserve identity without a permanent first-chapter commitment.
- Preact is the current frontend choice under the recorded bundle, composition, maintenance, and accessibility criteria; Phase 1 has explicit exit gates.
- Native JavaScript numbers are sufficient for the first slice only behind an adapter, finite checks, canonical serialization, and a migration threshold.
- A 60–120 active-equivalent-minute first Publication is the selected provisional band after re-running the pacing comparison; 60–90 and 90–120 are the strongest adjacent bands.

## Accepted with revision

- **Attention allocation:** use 3→4 whole slots, not the Foundation's six. Concavity applies only to production activities; project work is linear and uses a dedicated slot.
- **Resources and Technique:** use Precision and Intuition as the two primary stocks. Technique is retained as visible exercise/method preparation and typed step/template artifacts, especially through Constructive work, but is not a third stock, hidden efficiency score, or automation tax.
- **Projects:** projects do not consume Attention merely to run. Opportunity cost comes from reserved inputs, ordering, the single work slot, approach output, and downstream artifacts. Phase 1 must simulate the contrary competing-Attention project model before this becomes irreversible.
- **Branches:** use temporary project approaches rather than permanent Natural Numbers branches. Automation remains available to every approach.
- **Insight:** use deterministic stored charges with a provisional cap of 3, a 10–15% sustained target, and a 20% ceiling.
- **Offline:** use event-driven catch-up with a decision-aware stop and explicit safe policies. The 12-hour/full, 72-hour/maximum, 25% tail schedule is provisional and must be compared with alternatives.
- **Publication pacing:** target 60–120 active-equivalent minutes over 1–3 sessions, not the inherited 120–180-minute band.
- **Achievements:** keep seven as badge/history records and allow three non-power unlocks for records, visual information, and post-Publication challenge access. None may affect production, costs, caps, work, or formulas.
- **Storage:** use a dual local persistence layer: rotating validated `localStorage` for the current canonical save, settings, writer lease, and fallback backups; IndexedDB for append-only backup history and replay/diagnostic logs when available.
- **Frontend:** retain Preact + TypeScript + Vite, but reconsider if compatibility weight, accessibility behavior, or measured bundle/interaction budgets fail.

## Provisional values

The following are visible validated configuration, never hidden engine constants: starting/max Attention 3/4; activity exponent 0.80; Insight cap 3; active target 10–15% and ceiling 20%; offline 12-hour full window, 25% tail, and 72-hour maximum; first Publication 60–120 active-equivalent minutes; campaign 30–50 active-equivalent hours; approach-switch preservation floor 90%; all project costs, work, rates, caps, and approach modifiers. The machine-readable reconciliation register records a config path for every provisional cross-cutting value.

## Deferred

Global prestige purpose/timing/currency/formula/reset; permanent campaign branches; challenge play before first Publication; PWA/service worker; a large-number library; IndexedDB as the sole canonical store; audio implementation; localization; later-chapter data; and postgame remain deferred behind stated evidence gates.

## Rejected or superseded

- Six starting Attention; three primary Natural Numbers resource stocks; direct Precision↔Intuition conversion; Technique as an invisible efficiency meter; Technique as a universal-automation tax.
- Attention exponents 0.85/0.90 as settled facts, or any exponent applied to project work.
- Branch-exclusive automation, repeatable production clicking, random active popups, destructive random failures, and power-bearing achievements.
- A proven Real Numbers prestige point, Axiom currency, generic prestige formula, unlimited offline progress, or Publication as a multiplier reset.
- React selected only for ecosystem size, plain DOM selected only for bundle size, and unmeasured framework claims presented as fact.

## Risk of premature implementation

Hard-coding provisional values would turn comparative hypotheses into migration debt. Phase 1 should implement schemas, adapters, typed configuration, deterministic commands, both local persistence roles, the Natural Numbers data contract, and simulators capable of testing contrary models. Human comprehension, fun, pacing, approach identity, Technique visibility, and retention remain playtest questions.
