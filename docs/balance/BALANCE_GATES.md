# Balance gates

Critical gates for Phase 1/2 simulator data:

- all outputs finite/valid; no reachable value near adapter migration threshold without decision;
- all required content reachable and dependency graph acyclic (except explicitly modeled equivalence edges outside prerequisite DAG);
- every primary stock has ≥1 source, ≥2 meaningful sinks, and changes a policy-removal test;
- Technique method state has visible exercise/artifact sources and explicit matching sinks; it is neither a hidden modifier nor an automation gate;
- no one allocation or approach wins all representative policies/goals;
- dedicated project-slot and competing-Attention project models are compared before the selected model becomes costly to reverse;
- no approach is both fastest and best downstream;
- no mandatory clicking or active sustained advantage >20%;
- plausible opening mistake recovers within 5 minutes; no irreversible approach trap;
- no unexplained/hidden multiplier or effect from locked/unowned content;
- no chapter completion based only on one stock/score threshold;
- automation reduces manual actions and exposes rule trace; offline stopping is deterministic;
- every cap/queue/reserve boundary has tests; no circular conversion or stock with >80% unused time across viable policies;
- Publication ledger/state diff exact; all achievements have zero production power and any unlock is limited to records, visual information, or optional post-Publication access.

Failure blocks the relevant phase unless explicitly non-critical and deferred with owner, evidence need, and reversal plan.
