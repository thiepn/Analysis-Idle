# Art direction

## Compared directions

| Direction | Readability/mobile | Implementation/asset burden | Chapter extension/accessibility | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Living mathematical notebook / academic instrument | High: paper-like hierarchy, precise diagrams | Low–medium: CSS/SVG, restrained motion | High: chapter accent + diagram grammar; strong contrast possible | Can become generic productivity UI |
| Dark mathematical observatory | Medium: dramatic state lines | Medium–high: glow/motion tuning | Medium: contrast/low-vision and OLED bloom risks | Spectacle competes with planning |
| Modern academic publication / geometric lab | High: editorial typography/grid | Medium: more layout/illustration discipline | High, but formal tone may feel cold | Resembles a textbook/exam |

Selected: **living mathematical notebook / academic instrument**, blended with publication-quality typography. It best supports data density, procedural mathematics, mobile, reduced motion, and solo scope. Originality comes from state-driven proof marks, method cards, and chapter-specific diagram grammars—not decoration.

Principles: warm restrained surfaces; dark readable ink; one chapter accent; luminous color used sparingly for state; diagrams communicate mechanics; motion explains transitions and stops under reduced motion; no parchment cosplay, stock science imagery, or decorative equations.
