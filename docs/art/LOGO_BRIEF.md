# Logo brief

Communicate transformation from discrete proof steps into coherent understanding. The mark must remain recognizable at favicon size, work in one color/forced colors, avoid a generic standalone integral symbol, and pair with the full name.

Explore: proof nodes forming an A; an induction sequence becoming a continuous curve; nested intervals closing a gap; an epsilon neighborhood; a precise transformation mark. Avoid chalkboard clichés, random formulas, graduation caps, brains/lightbulbs, and ornamental infinity symbols.

Deliver later: horizontal/stacked/mark-only SVG, monochrome/inverse, safe area/minimum size, app-icon adaptation, accessible text treatment, and provenance/license record. Phase 0 selects no final logo.
