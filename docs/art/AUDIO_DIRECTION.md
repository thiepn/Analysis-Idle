# Audio direction

Final audio is intentionally deferred until the vertical slice proves feedback needs. Planned event hierarchy: quiet control confirmation; project start; project completion; milestone; blocked rule; Publication; optional ambient layer. No passive tick, currency-per-second sound, audio-only status, streak cue, or timing advantage.

Targets (`PROVISIONAL`): control <150ms, completion <700ms, milestone <1.5s, Publication <3s; consistent loudness; low repetition fatigue. Settings provide master/effects/ambient categories, mute, and reduced-sensory mode. Muting never hides information; visual/text/haptic-equivalent feedback remains.

Use original or clearly licensed sources only; record creator/tool/source/license/modifications. No music is commissioned/generated before campaign tone and session length are playtested.
